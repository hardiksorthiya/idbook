Harmonious Living: Inside an Open-Concept Modern Dining and Kitchen Suite

Dining and cooking share one calm room. A rounded marble table sits in the light, while the kitchen stays quiet behind matte cabinetry, olive tile, and a softly lit breakfast bar.

The Dining Table

  • Curved Contemporary Table: A rounded rectangular dining table with a polished white marble top and dark-stained timber legs anchors the center of the room.
  • Ergonomic Barrel Seating: Six tailored dining chairs featuring curved, supportive backrests in textured oat upholstery soften the linear sightlines of the room.
  • Organic Suspended Chandelier: A branching bronze pendant light with glowing glass orbs drifts overhead like a sculptural mobile, paired with a low-profile metallic ceiling fan for comfortable airflow.
  • Vibrant Place Settings: Layered place settings with teal and turquoise accent rim bowls inject a pop of color against the neutral stone surface.

The Kitchen

  • Vertical Green Kit-Kat Tiles: Deep olive finger tiles create a textural backsplash behind the preparation counters, providing vertical rhythm and an earthy contrast against smooth grey wall slabs.
  • Matte Flat-Panel Cabinetry: Seamless, handle-less under-counter drawers in matte cocoa brown keep the cooking zone uncluttered and visually quiet.
  • Backlit Fluted Breakfast Bar: An illuminated island peninsula features vertical fluting washed in warm under-counter LED cove lighting, offering a relaxed spot for casual morning coffee.
  • Floor-to-Ceiling Glass Bar Cabinet: A dark-framed display unit with warm integrated shelf lighting showcases glassware and curated spirits, adding depth and nighttime ambience.
