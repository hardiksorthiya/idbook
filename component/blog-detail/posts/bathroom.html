Modern Spa Serenity: Inside a Luxury Bathroom with Exotic Stone and Fluted Millwork

The bathroom is treated like a small spa. Light ash millwork floats off the floor, a teal stone slab carries the color, and the wet area stays clear of visible fittings.

Vanity and Mirror

  • Asymmetric LED Mirror: A custom capsule mirror featuring a softly rounded top corner and integrated perimeter illumination casts even, shadow-free lighting while adding an avant-garde silhouette to the wall.
  • Exotic Teal Stone Countertop: The floating counter is topped with a vibrant slab of sea-green, heavily veined exotic stone that brings an organic pop of oceanic color into the neutral scheme.
  • Floating Dual-Drawer Unit: Clad in vertical-grain ash veneer, the cantilevered vanity features a sleek ceramic vessel basin and warm under-counter LED cove lighting that enhances the sense of floor space.

Storage and the Wet Zone

  • Bespoke Tall Storage: A floor-to-ceiling linen tower in light ash wood integrates fine CNC-grooved fluting and an oversized, split-circle handle pull for understated elegance.
  • Curvilinear Floor Inlay: In the foreground, an intricate monochrome marble inlay of interlocking petal motifs breaks up the solid flooring and introduces fluid movement.
  • Concealed Fixtures: Flush ceiling-mounted rain shower fittings and linear slot drains maintain an uncluttered aesthetic throughout the wet zones.
