Our beginning

How IDBOOK started

Founder Alpesh Patel

When founder Alpesh Patel was remodeling a personal project, the work began the way these projects often do. Halfway through, the client was influenced by someone who said that hiring an interior designer would only lead to spending more on the home, and that remodeling on your own was the better path.

That moment stayed with him. Alpesh began to ask whether this was a common belief among ordinary people: that an interior designer takes a home out of budget. From that thought came a decision — to build a platform where any person can remodel a home within budget, without an interior designer.

That platform is IDBOOK.
