About IDBOOK

An online interior designer for every home and every budget

IDBOOK is a platform for every homeowner, from everyday homes to larger ones, who wants to remodel without hiring an interior designer. IDBOOK itself is your online interior designer and home professional, giving you every detail needed to complete the interior of your space.

Whether the home is modest or generous, small or large, the idea is the same: beautiful design should stay within reach, with the plans, models, and guidance to finish the work yourself.
