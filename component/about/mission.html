What drives us

Our vision and mission

Our Vision

“We design dreams and experiences.”

Our vision is to design for an ordinary person’s need for a well-furnished home within budget. We explain interior design in simple language that anyone can understand, even without a technical background in this field. IDBOOK is dedicated to crafting unique and responsive spaces that go above and beyond the standard building experience, and to the personal dream of a home within budget — an architecture extended.

Our Mission

“Every common man will have an enriching and well-furnished house within his budget.”

We operate at the progressive edges of our profession. Our mission is to provide guidance, knowledge, and awareness about this field. The final result of our work will always be a budget house. IDBOOK is a free online interior designer for people who cannot hire one, giving them a house interior within their budget — a social interior design website.

Mission Statement

  • To become the leader of innovative products and state-of-the-art execution.
  • To become the client’s first choice by using the finest materials and outstanding workmanship in interior execution.
  • To work to our fullest to satisfy each client’s desires and needs, with world-class interiors and furnishings.
  • Uniquely, we stop, look, and listen. Our observations become the basis for our actions.
